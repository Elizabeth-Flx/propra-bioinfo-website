# Comparison
Generally both classifications show rather similar results. There is a difference regarding 256b with CATH classifing as "mainly alpha" while SCOP classifies it as "All beta proteins".

It is important to rember that SCOP and CATH classify proteins in different ways. SCOP is based on the evolution and structure of proteins, while CATH maily uses structure for its classification. Thus there may be differences in the assignment of classes and folds between the systems.
